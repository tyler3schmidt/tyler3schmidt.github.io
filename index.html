About

I work on calibrated Bayesian methods for sequential decision-making and causal inference, advised by Prof. Nathan Wikle. My research verifies when flexible, computationally efficient Bayesian procedures retain the same asymptotic guarantees as classical efficient estimators, and applies that theory to problems in reinforcement learning and causal inference.

Establishes a Bernstein–von Mises calibration result for causal effects under stochastic interventions, verified for a soft BART prior.
